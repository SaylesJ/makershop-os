Router Bit Basics

V-Bits (60° vs 90°)

60-Degree: Best for deep, narrow cuts and fine, delicate lettering.

90-Degree: Best for shallow, wide cuts, large bold lettering, and chamfering edges.

Upcut vs Downcut

Upcut (Pulling Up): Clears chips fast. Good for deep pockets, but leaves a rough top edge.

Downcut (Pushing Down): Leaves a perfectly clean top edge. Good for thin materials or shallow pockets where chip clearing isn't an issue.

General Feed Rates

  • Softwood (Pine, Cedar): 80 - 120 IPM
  • Hardwood (Oak, Walnut): 60 - 90 IPM
  • MDF: 100 - 150 IPM

*Always adjust based on bit diameter and depth of cut.
